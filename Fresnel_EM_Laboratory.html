Research notebook

Problem statement

How do material impedance, polarization, incidence angle, and absorption control electromagnetic energy partitioning at a planar interface?

Model and assumptions

  • Planar, homogeneous, isotropic media with exp[i(k·r − ωt)] convention.
  • Boundary is at z = 0; tangential electric-field coefficients are reported.
  • Interface transmission is separated from subsequent absorption in medium 2.

Core equations

εeff = ε₀(εᵣ′ + iεᵣ″) + iσ/ω kz = √(k² − kx²) Yᴛᴇ = kz/(ωμ), Yᴛᴍ = ωεeff/kz r = (Y₁ − Y₂)/(Y₁ + Y₂), t = 2Y₁/(Y₁ + Y₂)
